Julavision · privacy

Privacy, plainly.

Effective September 30, 2026

Julavision is a creative studio in Phoenix, Arizona, run by Kenneth “Jula” Henry. This page covers julavision.net and our booking calendar, VisCal, at book.julavision.net. Questions or requests: hello@julavision.net.

What we collect, and why

  • When you contact us or book a call — your name, email, what you need, and anything you write in the form. We use it to reply, schedule and do the work.
  • When you book a session — the time you pick and your answers to the booking questions. VisCal sends the confirmation, the calendar invite and the Zoom link, and a reminder and follow-up by email.
  • When you pay — sessions are paid through PayPal and music licenses through Crossmint. They handle your card; we never see or store card numbers.
  • If you connect a crypto wallet — the address stays in your own browser until you disconnect. It reaches us only if you use it for delivery at checkout.
  • If you chat with Vision, our site assistant — the conversation is processed by Aminos, who run it. Please don't share anything sensitive there.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

Who handles it for us

Vercel hosts the site and VisCal. Supabase stores VisCal's bookings. Resend sends our email. PayPal, Crossmint and Zoom handle payments and meetings. Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces. Each only gets what it needs to do its part.

What stays on your device

The site keeps a few small things in your own browser: a copy of any form you sent us, your sound setting, a connected wallet address, and whether our assistant has already said hello. None of it is sent anywhere by itself, and clearing your browser's site data removes it. VisCal only sets a login cookie if you sign in, which only the studio does.

How long we keep it

As long as we're working together, and after that only what we need for records we're required to keep. Ask and we'll delete the rest.

Your choices

Email hello@julavision.net to see, correct or delete what we have about you, and we'll reply within 24 hours on business days. You can unsubscribe from any email by replying to it.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
