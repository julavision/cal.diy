Booking a call or session

  • The Vision Call is free. Twenty minutes on Zoom, no obligation.
  • Paid sessions are paid when you book. The price shown on the booking page is the price, in US dollars.
  • Rescheduling: any time up to 24 hours before the session, using the link in your confirmation.
  • Cancelling: paid sessions are non-refundable inside 24 hours of the start time.
  • Some sessions need preparation from you (a track link, the site to audit, your offer). Please send it before we meet — the booking page says what.

Projects and retainers

Projects start with a 50% deposit, with the balance due on delivery. Two rounds of revisions are included; more are $250 an hour. Retainers run three months, then month to month. Anything specific to your project is set out in its proposal, and the proposal wins where it differs from this page.

Music licenses

Tracks from the Library are licensed, not sold. What you can do with a track is set by the license tier you buy, as written on your license certificate.

Advice

We give creative, marketing and strategy advice. We don't give legal, financial or medical advice.

Changes

If these change, we'll update the date at the top. The terms in place when you booked apply to that booking.
